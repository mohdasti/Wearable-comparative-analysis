1 Introduction

This report compares daily health metrics from three wearable devices worn by the same person over approximately 33 days (June 20 – July 22, 2026, US Pacific time):

  • Whoop bracelet
  • Oura Ring 4
  • Withings ScanWatch

We examine four metrics: resting heart rate (RHR), heart rate variability (HRV), sleep duration, and daily steps. The analysis window is anchored to Whoop coverage. All code is shown inline so the workflow is fully transparent.

3 Data quality and exclusions

Known issues handled in the pipeline:

  • Whoop steps are manually transcribed from the app (not exported)
  • Withings sleep on 2026-06-20 is corrupt (zero stages) — excluded
  • Withings duplicate sleep on 2026-06-26 — kept longest session
  • Withings HRV absent in 2026 — HRV comparisons are Whoop vs Oura only
  • Withings sleep data ends 2026-07-18 (no Jul 19–22 nights)
  • Low step counts flagged (not dropped) for sensitivity analysis

8 Limitations

  • Single participant, ~33-day window — correlations are exploratory, not population-level inference.
  • Whoop steps were manually typed into an RTF note; gaps and low values reduce reliability.
  • Withings HRV is not present in the 2026 export; only Whoop–Oura HRV comparison is possible.
  • Withings sleep stops after July 18, shortening three-way sleep overlap.
  • Devices use proprietary algorithms; disagreement does not imply clinical inaccuracy without a reference standard (e.g., polysomnography, ECG).
  • All timestamps interpreted in America/Los_Angeles.
